Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Overview

GLP&ME is a personal medication tracking app. Your privacy is fundamental to how we built it. All your health data stays on your device.

Data We Collect

Your health data never leaves your device. Everything you enter — medication, doses, dose schedules, symptoms, weight entries, notes, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. We have no accounts, and we never receive your name, email address, or Apple ID.

A small amount of non-health data does leave your device, only for the purposes below:

  • Usage analytics (PostHog). Which screens you open, onboarding and paywall steps, plan selected, and purchase results, plus device model, iOS version, app version, and language. Events are tied to a random identifier generated by the app, not to you. We do not use location from your IP address, session recordings, or tracking across other apps or websites. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat). Subscriptions are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, a random app user ID, and the analytics identifier above so we can verify your subscription and measure trial and purchase conversion.
  • App Store ad attribution (Apple AdServices). If you installed GLP&ME from an Apple Search Ads ad, an Apple attribution token is sent to RevenueCat, which checks it with Apple to tell us which ad campaign led to the install. This does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, GLP&ME may read weight, nutrition, and activity data from HealthKit. This data is used solely to display information within the app and is never transmitted off your device. Apple Health integration is entirely optional.

GLP&ME may write weight data back to Apple Health if you manually log weight entries. This only occurs with your explicit permission.

Notifications

GLP&ME may send local push notifications to remind you of upcoming doses. These notifications are scheduled entirely on-device and do not involve any external server.

Analytics

GLP&ME uses PostHog for usage analytics (feature usage, onboarding, and subscription funnel). Analytics never include health information: no medication, doses, schedules, weight, symptoms, notes, or Apple Health data. We do not use advertising SDKs, the advertising identifier (IDFA), or cookies. To opt out, turn off "Share usage data" in Settings > Privacy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. The service providers above (PostHog for analytics, RevenueCat and Apple for purchases and ad attribution) process the limited non-health data described in this policy on our behalf. Your health data is never shared with anyone.

iOS Widgets

GLP&ME includes iOS widgets that display your next dose and treatment information. Widget data is shared between the app and widget using an App Group container on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Children

GLP&ME is not intended for use by children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated date.

Data Retention and Deletion

On your device. Your doses, symptoms, weight entries, notes, settings, and the data shown in the widget are stored only on your device. They stay there until you delete them. Deleting GLP&ME from your device permanently removes all of this data, along with any scheduled dose reminders. We have no copy of it and cannot recover it.

Deleting entries in the app. To delete a single dose, symptom, or weight entry, open the Journal, press and hold the entry, then tap Delete. The app does not currently offer a one-tap “delete all data” option. To remove everything, delete the app.

Apple Health. You can stop GLP&ME’s access to Apple Health at any time. In the Health app, go to Summary, tap your profile picture, then choose Apps > GLP&ME. You can also use Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > GLP&ME. Weight entries that GLP&ME saved to Apple Health belong to your Health data and stay there after you remove the app or revoke access. You can delete them in the Health app.

Usage analytics (PostHog). PostHog keeps analytics events for up to 1 year, then deletes them automatically. To stop sending analytics, turn off "Share usage data" in Settings > Privacy. This takes effect immediately and stays off until you turn it back on. Turning analytics off does not delete events that were already sent. If you reinstall the app, it creates a new random identifier and analytics are on by default. To ask us to delete analytics data already collected, email us at Connor0813@icloud.com.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat). Apple processes your subscription. You manage or cancel it in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. RevenueCat keeps a record of your purchases, linked to a random app user ID, so your subscription can be verified and restored. We keep that record while it is needed to provide your subscription and for our records. Apple’s own purchase records are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. To ask us to delete your RevenueCat purchase record and linked analytics data, email Connor0813@icloud.com. GLP&ME has no accounts, so please include the approximate date you subscribed and, if you have it, the order ID from your Apple receipt email. That helps us find your records. Deleting this record does not cancel your Apple subscription. Cancel it in Settings first.
